The challenge

Collect enough engineering detail without making the enquiry process difficult.

Industrial requests can include multiple technical items, documents, contact information, and different engineering disciplines. The interface needed to guide visitors while producing a structured request that could be reviewed accurately.

What I built

A guided quotation flow backed by durable records and private files.

01

Multi-step RFQ

Guided visitors through requirement details, multiple items, contact information, and final review.

02

Inline validation

Surfaced incomplete or invalid information at the point of entry instead of only after submission.

03

Private attachments

Stored uploaded engineering files in private Cloudflare R2 rather than exposing public object URLs.

04

Structured output

Persisted submissions in D1 and generated RFQ documents for review and follow-up.

Architecture

Cloudflare services handle persistence, protected files, and server-side workflow logic.

What I learned

A business website becomes more valuable when it captures structured operational data.

The project moved beyond static content into form architecture, validation, storage design, private file handling, server-side delivery, and deployment—while still keeping the customer journey simple.
