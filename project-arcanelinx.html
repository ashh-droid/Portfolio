The website

Present an industrial company clearly without overwhelming the visitor.

The website was designed around Arcanelinx’s industrial automation and engineering capabilities, with responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile. Company information, solutions, downloadable profiles, contact paths, and SEO metadata are organised so visitors can understand the business before deciding how to engage.

01

Responsive layouts

Built responsive pages and navigation using Next.js, React, and TypeScript.

02

Business content

Presented company capabilities and downloadable profiles through a customer-focused information structure.

03

Contact & discovery

Added contact validation and SEO metadata to support clearer enquiry paths and search visibility.

Key feature · Guided RFQ

The quotation workflow is a feature inside the company website.

The guided workflow helps visitors turn an engineering requirement into a more structured quotation request. It supports step-by-step requirement capture, validation, multiple items, attachments, and review before submission.

01

Guided input

Breaks the quotation request into understandable steps rather than relying on one long contact form.

02

Validation

Checks information during the workflow so incomplete details can be corrected before submission.

03

Requirements & files

Supports multiple requirement items and private file uploads for engineering context.
