The website

Presenting a technical industrial company without overwhelming the visitor.

The website needed to explain Arcanelinx’s industrial automation and engineering capabilities in a clear, professional way across desktop, tablet, and mobile. I structured the content around solutions, company information, downloadable profiles, contact paths, and search-friendly metadata so visitors could understand the business before deciding how to engage.

01

Responsive company experience

Designed the information architecture and responsive layouts for the company’s industrial solutions and business content.

02

Clear enquiry paths

Connected solution discovery, contact information, company documentation, and quotation requests into one consistent experience.

03

Business-ready content

Added downloadable company profiles, contact validation, SEO metadata, and production build validation.

Key feature · Guided RFQ

Turning a complex engineering requirement into a structured quotation request.

The RFQ workflow is an important feature inside the website. It guides visitors through requirement details, supports multiple items and private attachments, validates information inline, and produces a structured record for engineering review.

01

Multi-step quotation flow

Guided visitors through requirement details, multiple items, contact information, attachments, and final review.

02

Inline validation

Surfaced incomplete or invalid information at the point of entry instead of only after submission.

03

Generated RFQ output

Created structured quotation records and generated RFQ PDFs for review and follow-up.

What I learned

A company website can communicate clearly and still support a useful operational workflow.

This project combined responsive web development, information architecture, validation, cloud persistence, private file handling, secure delivery, SEO, and deployment. The RFQ feature adds practical business functionality without replacing the website’s primary role: presenting the company and helping potential customers understand its capabilities.
